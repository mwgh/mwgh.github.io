
## v1.3.1
### 23rd August 2026
* You can now **search** for acts from the timeline view.
* Improvements to **highlight labels and colours customisation**:
   * A new setting allows you to have these **automatically load** when changing to a new event;
   (NB if you've customised them on clashfinder.com for that event, this will override favourites in the app)
   * The 'defaults' button rotates through Clashfinder defaults (1..4) and a suggested set.
* The bottom navigation bar **highlights today's date** if it's within the schedule.
* Sharing the app is now possible via messaging apps as well as QR code.
* The event picker now makes itself look more obviously scrollable.
* An improved About dialog.
* A sprinkling of minor fixes and UI improvements.
 
## v1.3
### 10th August 2026
* Completely revamped **direct highlights sharing** from the beta:
    * share directly via **QR code** when your friend is nearby;
    * share using **messaging apps** (email, WhatsApp, SMS, iMessage*) when they're not.
* Friendlier initial **onboarding**, especially if you don't have a Clashfinder account.
* Faster **scrolling and zooming** in timeline view.
* Improvements to **'only show now or soon'** view scaling.
* Music acts now indicate when you've rated them.
* Better text sizing and display on certain Android devices
* Lots of minor fixes and UI improvements.
 
(*receiving highlights in iMessage may require iOS 27)

## v1.2
### 28th June 2026
* **Direct sharing** of your highlights with friends (beta).
* New **landscape itinerary view** (larger screens only): rotate your device to see details alongside the listings.
* Long-press on the alert icon to choose a **bespoke notice period** for that act/performance.
* When choosing an event in Settings, long-press on any entry in the list to show the event’s details (helps when there are **multiple Clashfinders** for an event).
* Alerts now include **start time** of the act/performance.
* **Better searching** where acts include non-English characters and punctuation.
* Highlights refresh buttons now on the relevant rows in Settings.
* Links to App/Play Stores (hint 5★ hint).
* Ability to clear specific previously viewed events (beta in v1.2.1).
* New landscape ratings view, showing comments (beta in v1.2.2, improved in v1.2.3).
* The usual fixes and subtle tweaks.

## v1.1
### 3rd June 2026
* New **ratings view** and improved list interface:
    * tap on the act/artist/performance, venue or event/festival to see other acts there/then;
    * filter a search to just these too;
    * jump to this view from an act‘s details view in the schedule, to see your previous ratings.
* Refreshing your highlights from clashfinder.com **merges** these with local ones.
* Support for **12-hour time format**, through Settings; by default using the device‘s system setting.
* Settings page i button ’throbs‘ when **schedule has been updated**.
* Long act names scroll (once, continuously, or not at all: configured in Settings).
* You‘ve had big screens, really big screens and small screens; now better layout on medium-small screens!
* Improvements to legibility in the day timeline (reduced breaking of names).
* Better handling of poor mobile/cellular data signal (from ‘in the field’ testing)
* Musicbrainz data enrichment is more reliable.
* Bug fixes, UI improvements (especially in dark mode)… and the red ‘now’ line shows up after midnight!

## v1.0
### 14th May 2026
* Events are now **stored with locally added highlights**, so you can switch between events without losing what you’ve flagged.
* A much improved **event picker** shows a list of what’s on over the next week by default.
* Enhancements to readability, including making better use of larger and medium-small screens.
* Better handling of device OS permissions for event alerts.
* User guide is now viewable at large device font sizes.
* Lots of minor tweaks and even fixes for a couple of reported bugs! Please do report them, as well as suggestions.

## v0.9
### 21st April 2026
* **Alerts!** Finally, a much-requested feature. Choose a preferred notice period, then tap the alarm clock icon to be notified before your unmissable acts are due to start. When the alert comes through (even when you’re not using the app), you can snooze it or tap it to see details of the act.
* Plus lots of other smaller improvements, as usual. If you like this *free* app, please feel free (!) to give it a 5-star rating!

## v0.8
### 2nd April 2026
* **Ratings.** Comment and score a performance after it’s underway; add ad hoc ratings for any historical performance; then see, compare and edit these from Ratings in the app menu.
* Truly **full-screen landscape schedule view** (the ‘now or soon’ button floats above this when the day being shown is today).
* Choosing an event is easier: recent ones are listed first, then sections for current, future and recent past events after searching.
* Having previously optimised for small screens, now some love for larger screen device owners: more efficient and legible use of the extra space.
* Hiding stages/venues and days is clearer and more responsive.
* Pinch-scaling the timeline is more responsive (uses ‘ghost scaling’).
* Better handling of landscape orientation in other pages.
* Better first guess of initial timeline scaling when a new event is selected.
* New icons, fixes and more!

## v0.5
